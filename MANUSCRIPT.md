## What I build

I build systems that put a person in control of their own data, money, and
presence — starting with myself.

**termux-site** — a self-hosted personal platform running on an Android
phone: net-worth tracking (assets minus debts), a timeshare marketplace, a
production and trades ledger with a breaker-guarded auto-buy engine, a bank
ledger, an elements and accountability page, a map of markets, and tools that
connect to live networks — BlockDAG testnet balances and contract calls,
and an EMV chip-card decoder. Login, sessions, and tamper-evident proof
documents included. It runs on Termux, served over Tailscale.

**blockdag-contracts** — Solidity contracts for the BlockDAG Primordial
Testnet: a greeting contract and a self-contained ERC-20 token, compiled,
tested, and shipped with a deploy kit.

**data-gov-starter** — a DCAT-US v1.1 open-data starter kit: a
validator-clean `data.json` template, a field validator, and publishing docs.
Built for civic tech and open-data work.
