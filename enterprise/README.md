# enterprise

Enterprise records structure: administration, records, finance, operations, technology, database, AI network, documents, archives, and security.

## Divisions

- `00_ADMINISTRATION/` — Governance of the enterprise: the rules, procedures, and agreements the business runs on.
- `01_RECORDS/` — People and correspondence records for the enterprise.
- `02_FINANCE/` — Money in, money out, and the reports that prove it.
- `03_OPERATIONS/` — Day-to-day delivery: projects, stock, contracts, and work orders.
- `04_TECHNOLOGY/` — Systems, code, and infrastructure the enterprise runs on.
- `05_DATABASE/` — Data stores: live, in development, backed up, and migrated.
- `06_AI_NETWORK/` — AI agents, their assignments, research, reports, and audit trail.
- `07_DOCUMENTS/` — Documents flowing in, out, and from templates.
- `08_ARCHIVES/` — Closed and historical material, kept for reference and audit.
- `09_SECURITY/` — Who can access what, what was checked, and how to recover.

Each subfolder holds a `README.md` describing what belongs there.
